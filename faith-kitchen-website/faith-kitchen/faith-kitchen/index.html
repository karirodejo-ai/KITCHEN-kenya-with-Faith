How do I order?+

Explore the menu, add your favorites to your bag, and review your choices. The website’s checkout is a local demo and does not send an order or take payment. For a real order, call Faith at 862-216-3024 to confirm availability and details.

Can I request pickup or delivery?+

You can select pickup or delivery in the order form and save your US ZIP code. Faith confirms delivery availability, timing, and pickup arrangements directly. Entering a ZIP code does not guarantee delivery coverage.

What comes in the lunch set?+

The lunch set pairs githeri with Kenyan chai for $16.00—a $2.00 saving on their combined regular price of $18.00. Add it to your bag from the menu’s lunch-set banner.

Can I ask about allergies or spice preferences?+

Please discuss allergies, ingredients, and dietary requirements directly with Faith before ordering. The demo form also has a notes field, but it does not send your request. Suitability and substitutions must be confirmed with Faith.

Where is Faith’s kitchen based?+

Faith’s kitchen is listed at 133 N Day St, Orange, NJ 07050, USA. Call 862-216-3024 to confirm availability and make arrangements before visiting.

What should we try for a shared meal?+

Explore the menu together: nyama choma with kachumbari, pilau, or a stew paired with chapati or ugali. Finish with Kenyan chai and mandazi. Ask Faith to confirm portions and quantities for your group.

How can I pay?+

After Faith confirms your order and final total, you can pay using Zelle to muriithifaith6@gmail.com or Cash App to $FaithMuriithi. Confirm the recipient in your app before paying. Payments happen outside this website; it does not process or verify transfers.
